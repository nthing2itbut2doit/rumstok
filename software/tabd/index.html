Tabd

Modern tablature editor

Overview

Tabd is a streamlined, browser-based tablature editor designed to make writing, editing, and organizing guitar and bass music simple and intuitive.

Open Tabd ↗
THE PROJECT

Modern Tablature Editing, Without the Complexity.

Tabd is a browser-based tablature editor built for guitarists, bassists, and musicians who want a straightforward way to capture and develop musical ideas. Traditional music notation software can introduce unnecessary complexity into what should be a natural creative process. Tabd takes a different approach, focusing on a clean workspace, intuitive editing, and the tools musicians need to turn ideas into structured tablature.

DEEPER LOOK

Design, capabilities & direction.

01

Modern Tablature Editing, Without the Complexity. — continued

Whether documenting a new riff, developing a complete composition, or organizing musical ideas for future reference, Tabd provides a focused environment that makes the process more accessible.

02

Built Around the Music

Tabd emphasizes simplicity without sacrificing functionality. Its interface is designed to keep tablature front and center, reducing distractions and allowing musicians to work efficiently.

As a web-based application, Tabd offers a convenient alternative to traditional desktop notation software, with an emphasis on accessibility, clarity, and ease of use.

03

A Focused Creative Tool

Tabd isn't intended to replace a full digital audio workstation or complex music production suite. Instead, it serves a specific purpose: making tablature creation and editing a more straightforward experience.

Developed by Rumstok Labs, Tabd reflects a practical approach to software design—building focused tools that solve real problems without introducing unnecessary complexity.
