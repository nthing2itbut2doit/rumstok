Craft9

Work. Organized.

Overview

Craft9 is a browser-based work order management platform designed to make organizing, assigning, tracking, and completing work straightforward.

THE PROJECT

Work Order Management, Without the Unnecessary Complexity.

Craft9 is a modern, browser-based work order management platform built to help organizations manage everyday operations with greater clarity, consistency, and control. From individual repair requests to recurring maintenance responsibilities, Craft9 provides a centralized place to create, assign, manage, and document work. Technicians can focus on the tasks in front of them, while coordinators and administrators maintain visibility into workloads, deadlines, and completed activity.

DEEPER LOOK

Design, capabilities & direction.

01

Work Order Management, Without the Unnecessary Complexity. — continued

Designed around a dependable operational foundation, Craft9 prioritizes straightforward workflows, organized information, and accountability—without forcing organizations into complicated processes they don't need.

02

Built Around the Way Work Gets Done

Work Order Management

Create, assign, prioritize, and track work orders through completion. Every work order maintains a clear scope, responsible assignee, due date, and operational history.

My Work

A focused workspace gives users immediate access to their assigned work, relevant deadlines, and the information needed to get started.

Scheduled Work

Manage recurring maintenance and routine responsibilities through configurable schedules that automatically generate work orders when they're due.

Labor & Documentation

Record time worked, add comments, and attach supporting files or photographs directly to work orders, keeping essential information together.

Search & Operational Visibility

Find current and historical work orders, filter by relevant criteria, monitor approaching deadlines, and maintain visibility across properties and personnel.

Administration & Accountability

Manage users, permissions, properties, locations, and work classifications through centralized administration, supported by detailed audit records.

03

Simple at the Core. Built to Expand.

Craft9 is designed around a focused work order management system that organizations can begin using without extensive configuration.

Its modular architecture also provides a foundation for additional capabilities, including asset management, parts and inventory, advanced reporting, and future integrations.

Organizations can start with the essentials and expand as their operational needs grow.

04

Designed for Real Operations

Whether coordinating facility maintenance, managing work across multiple properties, or organizing everyday service responsibilities, Craft9 is designed to make work easier to understand, manage, and complete.

Less time navigating software. More clarity around the work itself.

05

Development Status: In Development

Craft9 is currently being developed by Rumstok Labs. Its initial release is focused on delivering a complete, reliable work order management foundation before introducing additional modules and capabilities.
