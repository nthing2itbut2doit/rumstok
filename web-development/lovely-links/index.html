PROJECT CASE STUDY

Design, delivery & practical functionality.

01

The project

Lovely Links Permanent Jewelry is a mobile permanent-jewelry business serving customers through events, parties, pop-ups, and appointments. The website was designed to communicate the brand’s warm, modern style while keeping the next step simple for visitors.

Its visual direction uses cream, blush, restrained gold, and clean typography to complement the jewelry rather than overpower it.

02

A mobile-friendly customer experience

The public site introduces the offerings, explains how to request an appointment, and provides practical information such as aftercare and forms.

The “Get Linked” call to action supports an inquiry-based booking process: customers request an appointment and the owner follows up. It is not an automated appointment-scheduling engine.

03

Events and owner workflow

Events are an important part of the business, so the site is structured to highlight upcoming appearances when they are available.

An owner-friendly Event Creator and management workflow is part of the planned implementation, covering event details, photography, draft and published states, and management of past events. Its production availability should not be assumed until verified.

04

Delivery and scope

The project pairs an approachable public-facing identity with a practical direction for managing event content. It demonstrates how a custom website can reflect a small business’s personality without sacrificing clarity or ease of use.
