PROJECT CASE STUDY

Design, delivery & practical functionality.

01

The project

AV Construction & Maintenance needed a clear, professional online presence for residential construction and property maintenance services in Dayton and surrounding areas. The website presents the company’s work, explains its service categories, and gives prospective customers a straightforward way to get in touch.

The design balances project photography with practical information so that visitors can quickly understand the services and request an estimate or submit a general inquiry.

02

Public website experience

The responsive site organizes services into renovations and remodeling, general repairs and maintenance, interior improvements and finishing, and exterior construction and improvements.

A work gallery and direct contact paths help prospective customers move from browsing to making an inquiry without navigating an unnecessarily complex site.

03

Inquiry management

The implementation includes customer inquiry forms connected to a Cloudflare D1 database, with server-side handling, anti-spam measures, and email-notification infrastructure.

An owner-oriented administrative workflow is designed around reviewing submissions, tracking follow-up status, adding private notes, and retaining inquiry history. Administrative access and individual features should be verified against the live deployment before being represented as publicly available.

04

Delivery and scope

This is a custom business website with a purpose-built inquiry workflow—not a generic theme demonstration. The project shows how a relatively small business website can pair a polished public experience with practical behind-the-scenes tools.
